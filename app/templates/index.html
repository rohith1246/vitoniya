// Single-pass lookup in O(n) time
Map<Integer, Integer> seen = new HashMap<>();
for (int i = 0; i < nums.length; i++) {
    int need = target - nums[i];
    if (seen.containsKey(need)) return new int[]{seen.get(need), i};
    seen.put(nums[i], i);
}

Frequently Asked Questions

Full FAQ page →
What is VitoniyaEdu?
VitoniyaEdu is an independent educational platform developed by Vitoniya Labs, offering structured courses in software engineering and backend development.
How do I start learning?
Browse the Explore Courses page, create a free student account, and click Enroll on any published course.
Do I need an account?
You can view the course catalog and syllabi without an account, but you need a student account to enroll, open lessons, and save your progress.
Are courses free or paid?
All published courses and lessons on VitoniyaEdu are free to read, enroll in, and track.
Can I learn at my own pace?
Yes. All lessons are self-paced with no cohort deadlines.
How is progress tracked?
Inside each lesson, click Mark as completed. Your completion percentage and current chapter update automatically in your Learning Journal.
How do I contact support?
Visit our Contact & Support page or email info@vitoniya.com.
