Privacy Policy

VitoniyaEdu is operated by Vitoniya Labs (Hyderabad, Telangana, India).

1. Information We Collect

You can browse courses and read public lessons on VitoniyaEdu without creating an account. When you choose to register a student account, we collect your full name, email address, and a securely hashed password (stored with bcrypt), along with your course enrollments and lesson completion progress in our PostgreSQL database.

2. Session Cookies

We use an essential HttpOnly session cookie (vitoniya_session) solely to keep authenticated users signed in securely across requests. We do not sell or rent your personal account information to third parties.

3. Advertising & Analytics

VitoniyaEdu may display advertising through third-party networks such as Google AdSense to keep educational content free. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites. You can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through your Google Ads Settings.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

You may request updates or permanent deletion of your student account and learning records at any time by emailing info@vitoniya.com.
