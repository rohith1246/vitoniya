Frequently Asked Questions

Answers based on how VitoniyaEdu currently works.

What is VitoniyaEdu?
VitoniyaEdu is an independent educational platform developed by Vitoniya Labs, focused on structured software engineering and computer science tracks.
How do I start learning?
Create a free student account on the Signup page, open any course in Explore Courses, and click Enroll in this course.
Do I need an account?
Browsing the catalog and viewing course syllabi is open to everyone. A student account is required to enroll, read lessons, and save progress.
Are courses free or paid?
All currently published courses are free to enroll and study. [Configurable placeholder: update this policy if paid courses or subscriptions are added.]
Can I learn at my own pace?
Yes. All tracks are self-paced with no fixed deadlines.
How is progress tracked?
Each lesson includes a Mark as completed button. Your completion count and percentage are saved to your account and shown in your Learning Journal.
How do I contact support?
Reach us anytime via the Contact & Support page.
